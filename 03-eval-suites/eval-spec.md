## Part 2 · Three Audience Messages

### A. For Engineering (Jira ticket)
Acceptance Criteria: GIVEN a user asks about a product capability (export, integration, compliance, etc.) AND the response affirms that capability exists, WHEN the claim isn't backed by our current feature list, THEN block the response and return the fallback: "Let me confirm that with the team and follow up." Zero tolerance — test against 20 historical feature-capability queries, all 20 must match our actual feature list.

### B. For UX / Design
When the feature-claim guardrail blocks a response, show: "Let me confirm that with the team and follow up." No raw errors, no dead ends, and keep the rest of the conversation going normally.

### C. For Leadership (bi-weekly update)
We've put a guardrail on Ascend IQ to stop it from confirming product capabilities we don't actually have, after the audit caught it falsely claiming native SQL export support. This protects deals in the technical-eval stage from being built on a false premise, and reduces churn risk from customers who'd otherwise discover the gap post-signature. Tracking weekly.
